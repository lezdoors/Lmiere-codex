# Lmiere brand system

The source of truth is the boxed Bodoni **L** with the filled signal sparkle. It is deliberately editorial, technical, and slightly uncanny. Do not substitute the exploratory loop, monogram, or orbit marks.

## Primary use

- Paper surfaces: cobalt `#153E78`
- Night surfaces: signal `#86B875`
- Electric archive surfaces: electric `#1727FF`
- One-color production: night `#03100D` or white `#FBFAF4`
- Primary paper: `#EEE6D6`
- Accent only: sulfur `#E9AD00`

Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or detach the sparkle from the L.

## Typography

- Display: Bodoni Moda, Regular
- Interface and metadata: IBM Plex Mono, Regular or Medium

## Assets

Production-ready SVG and PNG files live in `public/brand`. The SVG files contain outlined vector artwork and do not depend on an installed font. Run `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs` to regenerate the raster sizes.
