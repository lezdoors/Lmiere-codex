# Lmiere showpiece direction

## Visual thesis

Lmiere is an impossible optical instrument documented by a precise field team. The landing page should feel recovered rather than retro: a complete technical artifact whose paper, type, registration marks, and specimen imagery prove craft, then wakes into a modern generation service. It refuses the category-standard gradient hero, model picker, and feature-card grid.

## Hero focal asset

Use the original field-machine illustration as the decisive first-viewport specimen. The nearby pixels bend in a restrained liquid swirl under a fine pointer; touch and reduced-motion users receive the complete static plate. The copy and primary action remain visible before motion initializes.

## Type hierarchy

- Bodoni Moda: oversized conceptual headlines and outcome names.
- IBM Plex Mono: navigation, price, status, controls, captions, and technical metadata.
- Mixed sizes create tension; mixed type systems do not. No third display family.

## Colour system

- Landing and marketing: warm bone paper with oxidized cobalt ink and sparing sulfur signal.
- Archive: electric ultramarine field with bone/white editorial content.
- Studio: a north-lit optical bench—pale mineral canvas, charcoal notation, cobalt action, and one restrained brass signal. It is deliberately calmer and brighter than the field manual.
- Account, wallet, and checkout: deep forest-black with readable pale green and sulfur reserved for state and price.
- Do not merge these palettes within one route.

## Section sequence

1. Field cover: one-line offer, exact action, focal machine.
2. Outcome proof: three outcome types with real prices and formats.
3. Live mechanism: prompt, price approval, and result shown as one connected sequence.
4. Phosphor transmission: one authored artwork demonstrating Lmiere's experimental range.
5. Private ownership: verified account, isolated wallet, recoverable archive.
6. Final field entry: open the studio, with login available but not required to understand the offer.

## Motion narrative

The instrument assembles once, then a persistent Roman-numeral field index exchanges six distinct full-screen chapters in place. Outcome plates rise in sequence; the mechanism draws laterally like an operating instruction; the Phosphor transmission opens from the center signal; ownership resolves into audited evidence; the final specimen enters as one closing plate. GSAP owns this choreography. No smooth-scroll engine is installed because chapter navigation does not scroll the document. Pointer warp remains local to the focal image. The brand star glints idly but never competes with the primary action. Reduced motion renders every element in its final state immediately, and the no-JavaScript frame remains complete.

## Rendering decisions

- Smooth scroll: none. The current chapter-stage architecture exchanges views in place and does not justify an additional engine.
- Three.js: no. It adds weight without strengthening the optical-instrument idea.
- Canvas: retained only for the existing local image warp and the single bounded Phosphor artwork.
- Commercial routes: no smooth-scroll choreography; use small CSS spring-like feedback and direct task states. The studio's only continuous motion is the interpretation lens while a run is active.

## Asset provenance

All showpiece imagery is supplied within this repository under `public/assets` or `public/brand`. Nous Research, Hermes Agent, and Psyche supply only hierarchy, pacing, contrast, image-treatment, and motion principles. No third-party reference assets, code, identity, or copy ship with Lmiere.
